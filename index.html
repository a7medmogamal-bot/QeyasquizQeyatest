How it works

From idea to result in three steps.

  1. 01

    Create

    Build your exam with multiple forms, images, timing, and access rules.

  2. 02

    Publish

    Share a secure link or QR code. Students open it and start instantly.

  3. 03

    Assess

    Objective questions grade automatically. Review, add feedback, and publish results.
